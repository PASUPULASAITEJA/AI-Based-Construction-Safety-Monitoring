Metal Smelting & Smelting Furnace

Heavy Industrial Foundry

Foundry worker standing in close proximity to an active smelting furnace with intense radiative heat and luminous particle glare.

Required PPE: Face-guard (shield), protective suit, insulated gloves, heavy safety boots.
Research Paper Finding (Section IV):
High-intensity flame glare challenges standard detectors. YOLOv9-e correctly demarcates the worker boundary and face shield without saturation blowouts.
